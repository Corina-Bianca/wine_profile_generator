# Ageing potential: still wines

Wine Label-to-Profile Skill (/wine-page) by Corina-Bianca Gonteanu, CC BY-NC 4.0.

Sparkling wines: see sparkling.md. Fortified wines: see fortified.md (each has its own bands).

Row: Ageing potential (Serving, storage and pairing). Counted from the vintage. The row shows a band only when a source states the potential; otherwise it stays blank. No dates are computed ("drink until 2032" is shown only if a source says so, in its words).

## Bands

under 3 / 3–5 / over 5–10 / over 10 years.

- A range uses its upper figure N (for example, "5 to 8 years" gives N = 8).
- Continuous threshold mapping on the upper figure N (in years):
  - N < 3 years: under 3 years
  - 3 ≤ N ≤ 5 years: 3–5 years
  - 5 < N ≤ 10 years: over 5–10 years
  - N > 10 years: over 10 years
- "Decades" maps to over 10 years.
- The value starts with the band and keeps the source's own wording after a colon: "over 10 years: decades of ageing potential".

## Readiness statements

When exactly one of the four WSET Level 3 readiness statements is supported by the source, it may be added to the value, in WSET's wording: too young; can drink now, but has potential for ageing; drink now: not suitable for ageing or further ageing; too old. If two or more could fit, or none is clearly supported, add nothing. (Wording checked against the WSET Level 3 Systematic Approach to Tasting, Issue 2, 2022. The WSET document is © WSET and is not included in this package.)

## Why bands

Sources give potential in many forms (a year, a range, "decades", "drink now"). Bands make them comparable across pages without inventing precision.

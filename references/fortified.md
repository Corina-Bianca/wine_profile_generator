# Fortified wines

Read instead of the still-wine rules for ageing potential and storage, and together with aroma-wheel.md for aroma and flavour.

## Ageing potential

Bottle ageing only. Cask or solera ageing stays in Winemaking under Maturation.

Bands: under 5 / 5–10 / over 10–20 / over 20 years. The band is shown only when a source states it.

- A range uses its upper figure N (for example, "10 to 15 years" gives N = 15).
- Continuous threshold mapping on the upper figure N (in years):
  - N < 5 years: under 5 years
  - 5 ≤ N ≤ 10 years: 5–10 years
  - 10 < N ≤ 20 years: over 10–20 years
  - N > 20 years: over 20 years
- "Decades" maps to over 20 years.
- Source wording is kept; no dates are computed.

Starting point, named in brackets whenever a source gives one:

1. "from vintage" for vintage-dated wines.
2. "from bottling" when a bottling date is given (Colheita, solera, undated styles).
3. Neither stated: figure as given, no starting point added.

"No bottle ageing intended" is shown only when a source says so (for example, best drunk soon after bottling). Never inferred from the style.

Example value: "over 20 years (from vintage): decades of ageing potential".

## Storage

Two lines when sources give them: closed bottle (temperature, position, light, directions as given) and after opening (how long it keeps and how, as given). Same priority order as every row. After-opening life is never inferred from the style.

## Aroma and flavour

Fortified wines use the still-wine wheel in aroma-wheel.md (Noble). Reason: it covers every fortified style (Port, Sherry, Madeira, Marsala, and others), while individual wheels each cover one style only and are producer or vendor material. All building rules in aroma-wheel.md apply unchanged.

## Style and sweetness

Style row carries the legally defined style or category as printed (Ruby, Tawny, Vintage, Fino, Oloroso, and so on) and sweetness as printed or as the source states. No figures inferred.
